Dig to discover

Digging reveals a brawler. Gold buys it. Two goals at once, so a dig is never wasted.

Section 05

Six ways to play

One arena mode, one boss fight, four sports. All of them run on the same skill.

You never carry the ball. You shoot it.

No pickup, no dribble, no possession. The ball is a physics object and your blaster is how you move it — passing is just shooting to a teammate. That single rule turns four sports into four aiming games, and it's what makes them ours rather than a sports sim with blobs pasted on.

Section 06

Party up, fair sides

Invite friends, pick your size, and the teams are always even — the game will not start 5v1.

What this is

This page is built from the build prompt, not from Sam's blob_brawl_dig.html — I haven't got that file, so nothing here is copied from it. It's a preview of how the Roblox version reads: the feel of the shooting, the five brawlers, the dig, the sports modes and the party rules, all running off the same numbers written in the doc.

  • Real in this preview: movement, aiming, shooting, dashing, all five supers, all 25 skin effects, digging to discover, crates, bushes, gold.
  • Sketched, not built: the sports fields, other players, saving to a Roblox account.
  • Everything here is original. No other game's names, art, sounds or screens — sports themselves belong to everyone, and blobs are ours.
